September 4

Even When You're Far Away

My love,

It's raining tonight, and rain always makes me think of you — I'm not sure why, except that quiet weather has a way of making the distance feel louder.

Some days you're right here in every plan I make. Other days there are miles between us, and all I have is a screen and the sound of my own thoughts. Tonight is one of those nights.

But here's what I want you to know: distance changes the shape of missing you. It never changes how much I do.

I still think about the small things — the way you laugh at your own jokes before you finish telling them, the voice notes I replay just to hear you again. None of that needs us to be in the same room to be true.

So wherever you are right now, however far that is from where I'm sitting — I hope it's warm. I hope you're safe. And I hope some small part of you can feel that I'm thinking of you, even through the rain.

With love,
Yours, Nihal
